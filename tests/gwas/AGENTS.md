## Contract tests

- `test_gwas_failed_association_tests.py`: a test that cannot be fitted is missing data (NaN statistics, `status`, `error`, `converged=False`) for the linear, logistic and mixed-model tests; Bonferroni, Benjamini-Hochberg, q-value and `adjust_p_values` leave it out of the family and keep outputs aligned, with `n_excluded`; the summaries (`n_variants_tested`, `n_tests_failed`), the writers (`NA`) and the workflows (`n_tests_failed`) skip it. Oracles: `scipy.stats.linregress`, `scipy.stats.false_discovery_control` and hand arithmetic.
- `test_gwas_zero_residual_variance.py`: a constant phenotype is a failed linear test with a message; an exact fit with a nonzero effect has t of plus or minus infinity and p = 0, never p = 1.
- `test_gwas_quality_count_validation.py`: `apply_qc_filters` raises `ValueError` up front for ragged genotype rows or a variant-record count that differs from the genotype columns, with and without `min_qual`.
- `test_gwas_workflow_summary_maf.py`: the workflows pass the genotypes to the writers, so the MAF column of `summary_statistics.tsv` and `significant_hits.tsv` matches hand-counted frequencies.
- `test_gwas_amellifera_maf_panel.py`: results carry `maf`, and the A. mellifera MAF panel is drawn from it and skipped, with a warning, when no result has one.
- `test_gwas_square_genotype_orientation.py`: `execute_gwas_workflow` declares `layout="sample_major"`; an undeclared square matrix is treated as variant-major with a logged warning.
- `test_gwas_seeded_randomness.py`: `calculate_ld_decay`, `ld_decay_plot`, `ldsr_plot` and `subsample_convergence` take `seed`: same seed, same output whatever the global state; different seed, different draw; the global `random` state is left untouched.
- `test_gwas_analysis_eqtl.py`: `run_eqtl_analysis` with `variant_positions` and `gene_positions` tests only cis pairs (same chromosome, `|position - TSS| <= cis_window`, including pairs on the window edge), adds `distance`, and rejects a `cis_window` without positions; effects match `scipy.stats.linregress`.
- `test_gwas_credible_set_ld_contract.py`: the `ld_matrix` of `compute_credible_set` holds correlations, and the log Bayes factor is lowered by `log(1 - r**2)` with the floor at 0.01.
- `test_gwas_finemapping_regional_coloc_errors.py`: `regional_coloc` reports `n_failed_windows`, chooses `best_window` among the windows that succeeded and returns `status: "error"` when every window fails.
- `test_gwas_download_variant_data_contract.py`: `download_variant_data` raises `NotImplementedError` for `"vcf"` and `"both"` and `ValueError` for an unknown type before touching the disk or the network (the module points the proxy variables at a closed local port).
- `test_gwas_generate_all_plots_inputs.py`: `generate_all_plots` reads lists, DataFrames and `.json`, `.tsv`, `.txt` and `.csv` files under every spelling of chromosome, position and p-value, leaves out results without a p-value, raises on unsupported input and closes its figures.
